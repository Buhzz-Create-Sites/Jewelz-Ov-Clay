    Born in a
    Pandemic.
    Built with Clay.

    During the lockdown of 2020, creativity became survival. Jewelz Ov Clay was born when one artist turned isolation into art — hand sculpting, painting, and baking bold polymer clay jewelry right here in Philadelphia. The "Ov" in our name? Every piece goes straight into the oven. That's not just a name. That's a promise.
