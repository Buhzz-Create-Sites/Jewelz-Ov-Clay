✦ Our Story

Born in a
Pandemic.
Built with Clay.

During the lockdown of 2020, when the world went quiet, one artist in Philadelphia got loud. Jewelz Ov Clay was born from isolation, creativity, and a refusal to be still. What started as a way to fill the silence turned into something the world needed to wear.

The "Ov" in our name? Every piece goes straight into the oven. Hand sculpted, hand painted, hand baked — right here in Philly. No factory. No duplicates. No compromise. Just bold, colorful wearable art made for people who refuse to blend in.

✦ How It's Made

From Clay to Crown

Every piece goes through the same four steps. Every single time.

01
🎨
Mix & Sculpt

Raw polymer clay is hand blended into bold custom color combinations — swirls, marbles and patterns that can never be exactly replicated.

02
✂️
Shape & Form

Each piece is hand cut and shaped into hoops, teardrops, hexagons, diamonds and hearts. No molds. No machines. Just hands.

03
🔥
Baked "Ov"

Every piece goes into the oven — hence the name. Heat sets the clay and locks in the colors permanently. This is where the magic gets sealed in.

04
💎
Finished by Hand

Beads, charms, hooks and chains are added by hand. Each earring is inspected, finished and packaged as a one of a kind original.
